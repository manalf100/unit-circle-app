1. Core Mathematical Laws & Advanced Formulas

  • Point-Slope Form: \(y - y_0 = m(x - x_0)\)
  • Tangent Slope Law: \(m_t = \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=x_0}\) (Blue Line)
  • Normal Slope Law: \(m_n = -\frac{1}{m_t} \quad (m_t \cdot m_n = -1)\) (Red Line)
  • Implicit Differentiation: \(\frac{d}{dx}[F(x, y)] = 0\)

3. STEM-Level Solved Examples

Example 1: Find equations of tangent and normal to \(x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 4y = 0\) at \(P(3, 3)\).
Solution: \(m_t = -2 \implies \text{Tangent: } 2x + y - 9 = 0 \text{ & Normal: } x - 2y + 3 = 0\).
Example 2: Find points on \(y = x^3 - 3x\) where the tangent line is parallel to the x-axis.
Solution: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 3 = 0 \implies x = \pm 1\), yielding points \((1, -2)\) and \((-1, 2)\).
