1. Core Mathematical Laws & Advanced Formulas

  • Point-Slope Form: \(y - y_0 = m(x - x_0)\)
  • Tangent Slope Law: \(m_t = \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=x_0}\)
  • Normal Slope Law: \(m_n = -\frac{1}{m_t} \quad (m_t \cdot m_n = -1)\)
  • Implicit Differentiation: \(\frac{d}{dx}[F(x, y)] = 0\)

3. STEM-Level Solved Examples (Step-by-Step)

Example 1 (Implicit Differentiation & Tangent/Normal): Find the equations of the tangent and normal lines to the circle \(x^2 + y^2 - 2x - 4y = 0\) at \(P(3, 3)\).

Step 1 (Differentiate): Apply implicit differentiation: \(2x + 2y\frac{dy}{dx} - 2 - 4\frac{dy}{dx} = 0\).
Step 2 (Find Slope): Substitute point \((3, 3)\) to get \(6 + 6\frac{dy}{dx} - 2 - 4\frac{dy}{dx} = 0 \implies m_t = -2\).
Step 3 (Tangent Equation): \(y - 3 = -2(x - 3) \implies 2x + y - 9 = 0\).
Step 4 (Normal Equation): Since \(m_n = \frac{1}{2}\), then \(y - 3 = \frac{1}{2}(x - 3) \implies x - 2y + 3 = 0\).
Example 2 (Parametric & Higher-Order Thinking): Find all points on the curve \(y = x^3 - 3x^2\) where the tangent line is horizontal.

Step 1: Find the first derivative: \(\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 6x\).
Step 2: Set derivative to zero for horizontal tangent: \(3x(x - 2) = 0 \implies x = 0 \text{ or } x = 2\).
Step 3: Find corresponding y-coordinates: at \(x = 0 \implies y = 0\); at \(x = 2 \implies y = -4\).
Step 4: Points are \((0, 0)\) and \((2, -4)\).
