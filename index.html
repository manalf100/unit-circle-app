1. Core Mathematical Laws & Formulas

  • Point-Slope Form: \(y - y_0 = m(x - x_0)\)
  • Tangent Equation Law: \(y - y_0 = m_t(x - x_0) \quad \text{where} \quad m_t = \left.\frac{dy}{dx}\right|_{x=x_0}\)
  • Normal Equation Law: \(y - y_0 = -\frac{1}{m_t}(x - x_0) \quad (\text{Condition: } m_t \cdot m_n = -1)\)

2. Step-by-Step Solved Examples: Tangent & Normal

Example 1 (Polynomial Curve): Find the equations of the tangent and normal lines to \(y = 2x^3 - 5x + 3\) at \(x = 1\).

Step 1: Find point \(P(1, 0)\).
Step 2: Slope \(m_t = 6x^2 - 5 \implies m_t = 1\).
Step 3: Tangent Equation: \(y - 0 = 1(x - 1) \implies y = x - 1\).
Step 4: Normal Equation: \(y - 0 = -1(x - 1) \implies y = -x + 1\).
Example 2 (Exponential Growth): Find the equation of the tangent line to \(y = x \cdot e^x\) at \(x = 0\).

Step 1: Find point \(P(0, 0)\).
Step 2: Derivative \(\frac{dy}{dx} = e^x + x e^x \implies m_t = 1\).
Step 3: Tangent Equation: \(y = x\).

3. Step-by-Step Solved Examples: Higher-Order Derivatives

Higher-Order Example 1: Find the 3rd derivative of \(f(x) = \frac{1}{x} = x^{-1}\).

1st Deriv: \(-x^{-2}\) | 2nd Deriv: \(2x^{-3}\) | 3rd Deriv: \(f'''(x) = -\frac{6}{x^4}\).
Higher-Order Example 2: Find the 2nd derivative of \(f(x) = \ln(2x + 3)\).

1st Deriv: \(\frac{2}{2x + 3}\) | 2nd Deriv: \(f''(x) = -\frac{4}{(2x + 3)^2}\).
