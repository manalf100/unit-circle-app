Topic: Parametric & Advanced Chain Rule Differentiation

1. Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ if $x = t^2$ and $y = t^3$.

2. Find the derivative of $\sqrt{5 + x^2}$ with respect to $\frac{x}{x+2}$ at $x = 2$.

3. Find the slope of the tangent line to the parametric curve $x = 3\cos(t)$, $y = 3\sin(t)$ at $t = \frac{\pi}{4}$.

4. If $x = e^t \cos(t)$ and $y = e^t \sin(t)$, find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ at $t = 0$.

5. Find the second derivative $\frac{d^2 y}{dx^2}$ given the parametric equations $x = t^2$ and $y = t^3$.
