# أسئلة التمارين (MCQ) - STEM Grade 12 (Implicit Differentiation)

1. Find dy/dx if x^2 + y^2 = 25 at point (3, 4):
   a) -3/4
   b) 3/4
   c) -4/3
   d) 4/3

2. What is the derivative of sin(y) with respect to x?
   a) cos(y)
   b) -cos(y) * (dy/dx)
   c) cos(y) * (dy/dx)
   d) -sin(y) * (dy/dx)

3. If x * y = 6, find dy/dx:
   a) -y/x
   b) y/x
   c) -x/y
   d) x/y

4. Find the slope of the tangent line to x^3 + y^3 = 2xy at (1, 1):
   a) 1
   b) -1
   c) 0
   d) Undefined

5. If e^(xy) = x + y, what is dy/dx at (0, 1)?
   a) 1
   b) 0
   c) -1
   d) 2

6. Derivative of ln(xy) implicitly with respect to x:
   a) (1/x) + (1/y)(dy/dx)
   b) (1/x)(dy/dx) + (1/y)
   c) (1/xy)
   d) None of the above

7. Find dy/dx for x^2 - y^2 = 16:
   a) x/y
   b) y/x
   c) -x/y
   d) -y/x

8. If tan(y) = x, then d^2y/dx^2 is:
   a) -2x
   b) -2x / (1+x^2)^2
   c) 2x / (1+x^2)
   d) 1 / (1+x^2)

9. Implicit differentiation of x^4 + y^4 = 17 gives dy/dx equals:
   a) -(x^3)/(y^3)
   b) (x^3)/(y^3)
   c) -(y^3)/(x^3)
   d) (y^3)/(x^3)

10. At which point on x^2 + 4y^2 = 4 is the tangent horizontal?
    (Hint: dy/dx = 0)
    a) (±2, 0)
    b) (0, ±1)
    c) (1, 1)
    d) (0, 0)
