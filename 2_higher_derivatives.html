2. STEM Exam-Style Solved Examples (Critical Thinking & Concept Integration)

Example 1 (Implicit Differentiation & Perpendicularity): Find the equation of the normal line to the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 25$ at the point $(3, 4)$.
Concept: Use implicit differentiation to find the slope of the tangent, then find the negative reciprocal for the normal line passing through the given point.
Example 2 (Parameter Solving): Find the value of $k$ if the tangent line to $y = kx^3 - 4x$ at $x = 1$ is perpendicular to the line $2x + 6y = 11$.
Concept: Relate the derivative at the given point to the negative reciprocal of the given reference line's slope.
Example 3 (Higher Order & Tangency): Find the points on the curve $y = x^3 - 3x^2$ where the tangent line is horizontal ($m_t = 0$).
Concept: Set the first derivative equal to zero and solve for $x$ coordinates of local extrema.
Example 4 (Common Tangent Concept): Find the equation of the tangent to $y = x^3 - 3x$ that is parallel to the line $9x - y = 5$.
Concept: Equalize the derivative to the slope of the parallel line ($m = 9$) to find the point of tangency first.
Example 5 (Product Rule & Normal): Find the slope of the normal line to $y = x e^x$ at $x = 0$.
Concept: Apply the product rule for differentiation before evaluating the slope at the specified point.
