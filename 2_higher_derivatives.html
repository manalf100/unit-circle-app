2. Five Step-by-Step Solved Examples

Example 1: Find the slope of the tangent to $y = x^3 - 3x$ at $x = 2$.
Solution: $\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - 3$. At $x = 2$, slope $m_t = 3(2)^2 - 3 = 9$.
Example 2: Find the slope of the normal line to $y = x^2 - 4x$ at $x = 3$.
Solution: $\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - 4$. At $x = 3$, $m_t = 2(3) - 4 = 2$. Normal slope $m_n = -\frac{1}{m_t} = -\frac{1}{2}$.
Example 3: Find the equation of the tangent line to $y = x^2$ at point $(1, 1)$.
Solution: $m_t = 2(1) = 2$. Equation: $y - 1 = 2(x - 1) \Rightarrow 2x - y - 1 = 0$.
Example 4: Find the equation of the normal line to $y = x^2$ at point $(1, 1)$.
Solution: $m_t = 2$, so $m_n = -\frac{1}{2}$. Equation: $y - 1 = -\frac{1}{2}(x - 1) \Rightarrow x + 2y - 3 = 0$.
Example 5: Find the value of $k$ if the tangent to $y = kx^3 - 4x$ at $x = 1$ is perpendicular to $2x + 6y = 11$.
Solution: Given line slope is $-\frac{1}{3}$, so perpendicular slope is $3$. $\frac{dy}{dx}\big|_{x=1} = 3k - 4 = 3 \Rightarrow 3k = 7 \Rightarrow k = \frac{7}{3}$.
